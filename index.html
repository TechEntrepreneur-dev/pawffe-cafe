Our Philosophy

A place where you don't have to leave your pet behind.

Located on the serene Beach Road of Endada, Pawffe is more than just a café—it's an escape into a calm beachside lifestyle. We created this space believing that slow mornings, great coffee, and meaningful moments are best shared with our four-legged companions.

Whether you're looking for a comfortable long-stay environment to work remotely, a picturesque spot for a weekend brunch, or simply a place to unwind while your pet relaxes by your side, Pawffe offers a premium, lifestyle-oriented experience tailored for you.
